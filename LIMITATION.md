## Known Limitations
 
This is a proof of concept (PoC) built on third-party services. Some limitations below come from the data sources rather than from the application's own logic.
 
### Shopee data
 
| Limitation | Details and how the app handles it |
|---|---|
| **Buyer reviews are not always available** | Reviews are collected by a community Actor on Apify. Shopee may block datacenter IPs on the review endpoint; when that happens the Actor returns only a summary row, with no review text. The app reports this clearly, still shows rating statistics when available, and the AI report **does not analyze reviews and does not invent praise or complaints**. A residential proxy may help (`APIFY_REVIEWS_PROXY_GROUP`), but this has not been verified on every Apify plan. |
| **No per-shop sales volume or voucher data** | Shopee does not expose these figures to logged-out visitors. The "Best selling" option relies on Shopee's own ranking, not on actual unit sales. Prices shown are list prices, before vouchers and shipping fees. |
| **Shop type is a guess** | The data source does not return Shopee Mall / Preferred badges. The "Official (guessed from name)" label is based only on the shop name and is **not** an official confirmation. |
| **Flash Sale and Freeship may be missing** | Both are detected from the product's promotion labels (`labels`); if the source returns no label, the app cannot know. |
| **Data is a snapshot** | Prices and ratings can change right after a scan. Results are cached for a few minutes (5 minutes for search, 10 minutes for reviews). |
 
### Algorithms and AI
 
| Limitation | Details |
|---|---|
| **The off-topic filter is heuristic** | The app keeps listings whose title contains the brand and most of the distinctive keywords, and drops accessories (cases, covers, etc.). It can drop valid items or keep near-identical variants (for example, the same line but a different generation). Check the product name in the table before buying. |
| **The "abnormally low price" flag does not prove a counterfeit** | The flag triggers when a price is below 35% of the median and the listing has few ratings. It is a warning signal, not a conclusion. |
| **The AI report is for reference only** | The report is generated by a language model (Gemini) from the collected data and may be wrong or incomplete. It is **not purchase advice**. |
| **Product recognition from images can be wrong** | The search keyword is inferred by AI from the image or description; if it is wrong, every later step is skewed. |
| **Simulated data mode** | Fake data, for illustrating the interface only, and off by default. When enabled, the app labels it clearly and the AI does not recommend a purchase. |
 
### Dependencies and operations
 
- **Third-party services:** community-maintained Apify Actors may change behavior, change pricing, or be discontinued at any time; Shopee may also change its anti-scraping measures.
- **Cost:** each scan consumes Apify credit and Gemini calls; scanning more products costs more. Users enter their own keys, so costs are charged to their own accounts.
- **API keys:** keys live only in the user's session and the app does not intentionally store them. Never commit a `.env` file to the repository.
- **Personal data:** Shopee reviews may contain buyers' display names. The app uses only the review text and star rating, and does not persist them.
- **Terms of use:** users are responsible for complying with the terms of Shopee, Apify and Google, and with applicable data-protection rules.
### Possible improvements
 
- Use a more reliable review source (a residential proxy or another Actor) and add fallbacks across multiple sources.
- Add sales volume and voucher data if a legitimate source becomes available.
- Replace the heuristic filter with more precise product-model matching (for example, AI-assisted title matching).
- Add automated tests for the normalization and ranking functions.

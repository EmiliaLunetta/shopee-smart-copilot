> Proof of Concept (PoC) built for the **Sea Global Management Associate Program (Sea GMAP 2027) - Shopee & Monee Track**.

## 📌 Problem Statement (Vấn đề người dùng)
Online shoppers often spend 15–20 minutes comparing identical items across hundreds of sellers, struggling with fake reviews and price discrepancies. 

## 🚀 The Solution (Giải pháp AI)
An AI-powered copilot integrating **Google Gemini Multimodal Vision** with **E-commerce Intelligence**:
- **Visual Product Recognition:** Automatically detects product category, brand, and model from uploaded images.
- **Smart Sorting & Filtering:** Ranks purchasing options by lowest price, highest ratings, or sales volume.
- **Synthesized Sentiment Analysis:** Extracts common pros and cons from real buyer feedback to produce an actionable buying verdict.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend / Deployment:** Streamlit Community Cloud, Python
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API (Multimodal Vision & NLP)
- **Data Architecture:** REST APIs, Python-dotenv, Pillow

## 📊 Business Impact for Shopee
- Increases **Conversion Rate (CVR)** by slashing consideration time.
- Lowers **Return & Cancellation Rates** by preventing purchases from low-quality sellers.
